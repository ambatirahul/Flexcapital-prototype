Assumptions & product principles

What this prototype represents — and what a production system would require.

Customer

Fictional SME — Northstar Commerce, cross-border B2B ecommerce/wholesale.

Modes

First-party = direct Airwallex relationship. Embedded = same underwriting logic surfaced inside a partner platform (e.g. ShipHub Commerce), using partner-sourced data instead of an existing Airwallex account.

Data

All financial figures are synthetic and user-editable for demo purposes.

Financing

All limits, rates and fees shown are illustrative.

AI layer

"Ask Capital Intelligence" uses deterministic demo logic, not a live model.

Credit decision

Not performed by this prototype.

A production system would also require

  • Customer consent (and, for embedded, partner data-sharing agreements)
  • Transaction/account data
  • Underwriting
  • Risk policy
  • Compliance checks per market
  • Lender/capital-provider integrations
  • Partner revenue-share/referral economics (embedded)
  • Pricing
  • Servicing
  • Repayment management
  • Regulatory controls

Product principles

"Growth cannot be separated from risk."
"Give customers transparency before asking them to apply."
"Centralize reusable lending capabilities while keeping market-specific policy and compliance modular."
"Optimize for risk-adjusted customer and business value, not application volume alone."
