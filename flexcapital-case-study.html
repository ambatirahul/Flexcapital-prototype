FlexCapital: Working Capital for Thin-File Merchants

A product concept for Airwallex Lending — case study

Problem

Two gaps surfaced from customer reviews and public reporting on Airwallex's lending experience: (1) thin-file exclusion — standard eligibility requires 90+ days of history, excluding fast-growing newer businesses that most need capital; (2) opaque cost of capital — lenders often disclose only an APR range after underwriting, not the true total repayment, eroding trust already strained by unexplained fund holds and slow support escalation.

Hypothesis

If eligibility runs on live cash-flow signal (deposit consistency, repeat-customer rate, revenue trend, currency diversification) instead of account tenure, and the full cost of capital is disclosed before acceptance, Airwallex can extend credit to an excluded segment, differentiate on trust, and do this at acceptable risk — because Airwallex already has first-party visibility into the revenue an advance is repaid from.

Solution

StageWhat it does
EligibilityCash Flow Velocity Score (0–850) replaces the 90-day tenure gate
OfferFull total repayment figure and flat cost rate up front — no APR range
TermsEvery material term on one screen
RepaymentDaily holdback tied to real revenue, live dashboard, human escalation path
SafeguardsMinimum floor + outside date bound the obligation once revenue stalls

Why this is viable, not just merchant-friendly

Revenue-share repayment has no defined end if revenue drops — FlexCapital bounds it with layered mechanics: an early-warning trigger (40%+ WoW drop) prompting a specialist check-in; a minimum weekly floor once revenue stays low for 10+ days; an outside date converting any remaining balance to a fixed installment plan; risk-based sizing at underwriting, so volatile-revenue merchants get a smaller advance and higher floor from the start; and true business closure priced into the portfolio-level flat rate, the same mechanism any unsecured lender uses.

Success metrics

Approval rate among sub-90-day accounts (at a defined default-rate ceiling), time-to-funds, repayment completion rate vs. outside-date conversion rate, cost-of-capital comprehension (offer-screen drop-off as a proxy for trust), and support escalation rate/resolution time.

Key risks

Revenue drops or stops — mitigated by the early-warning trigger, floor, and outside date above.

Adverse selection — volatile businesses seeking this specifically for its forgiveness — mitigated by Velocity Score sizing tied to consistency, not just growth.

Business closes before repayment — priced into the portfolio-level flat rate; not solvable per-loan.

Gaming live-data underwriting — mitigated by pulling score inputs from settled transaction data, not self-reported figures.

Prototype and case study built as a product management portfolio exercise, not an Airwallex work product.
