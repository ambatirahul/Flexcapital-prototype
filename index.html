A flexible rate isn't an open-ended one

9% of daily revenue protects Loom & Co. on a slow day. These three mechanics stop it from meaning "never" on a slow year.

Revenue drop trigger40%+ week-over-week

Fires an automatic check-in from a lending specialist — before any floor or fixed payment kicks in. Most slowdowns get resolved here.

Minimum weekly floor$150 / week

If daily revenue stays under $300 for 10+ consecutive days, the holdback switches from "9% of revenue" to this fixed minimum — small enough to survive a real slump, but the balance still moves.

Outside dateMonth 9

Whatever's still owed converts to a fixed installment spread over the next 3 months. The revenue-based rate is the default path, not the only one — there's always a defined end.
